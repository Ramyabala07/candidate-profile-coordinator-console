The brief

A coordinator checking one candidate's result

Coordinators answer candidates all day: "How did I do?", "Where did I lose marks?". To answer, they open the candidate's profile and look at the Result card.

4 sectionsStatistics, Machine learning, Data wrangling & SQL, Python
3 topics in eachFor example, Statistics has Descriptive statistics, Probability and Hypothesis testing
Scores as marksEach section is out of 25, each topic out of 8 or 9
Topics on demandEach view shows sections first; you open a section to see its topics

In the next three steps you'll see the same kind of result shown three different ways. Explore freely: hover, click and open sections. There are no wrong answers about the design, only about the data.
