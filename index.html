The two surfaces

They answer different questions, which is why there are two.

Enrollment profile — “where is this enrollment, and what’s blocking it?”

Opened from the enrollments list, scoped to one enrollment. A stage ladder with a status derived from the stages, evidence one level down. Timeline and Cards are two structures over the same data — same header, same tabs, same switcher, same evidence viewers.

Candidate workspace — “someone escalated; show me everything in flight”

Full page, candidate-scoped. Built around cross-enrollment comparison, which a 548px panel cannot physically do. Separates decisions that need you from flags that don’t, and shows identity continuity across every exam.
